cellular stories

I'm now excited to explore a model that labels the chemicals passed between cells as “cellular stories”, based on a definition of storytelling as "a method for sharing complex information about an inner world with other agents sharing an outer world."

This analogy is motivated by observations that stories have demonstrated great power for inspiring massive, long-lasting coordinations amongst humans -- similar to how our bodies, and the bodies of every multi-cellular organism, are living testaments of our cells' abilities to effectively coordinate as massive collectives. These coordinations can span distances many times larger than a cell, and can last many lifetimes longer than the lifespan of any individual cell.

At this moment, our global superorganism is especially dysfunctional and self-destructive. By combining studies of BIPOC liberationist storytellers such as Octavia Butler, bell hooks, and adrienne maree brown with an exploration of cellular strategies for collective actions, I hope to gain fresh ideas for building healthy, sustainable, compassionate, and resilient human and beyond human communities.
